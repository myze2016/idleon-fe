Match the reference assassin room palette and embroidered leaf details. Exact row-major order: row 1: (1) hooded assassin head bust for Character, (2) leather coin pouch with gold coins for Shop, (3) glowing violet crystal relic for Artifacts, (4) buckled travel backpack for Inventory. Row 2: (5) three small adventurer silhouette busts grouped for Team, (6) brilliant violet wishing star for Wish, (7) open silver-edged lore book for Wiki, (8) two crossed silver swords for PvP. Row 3: (9) ornate silver cogwheel with violet center for Menu, (10) unfurled parchment map with purple route and compass for Map, (11) right-pointing silver arrowhead with violet magical trail for Start adventure, (12) silver X with violet accents for Close. All icons independent isolated cutouts, real alpha transparent background between symbols, no room, no UI mockup. Precise regular grid is essential for runtime sprite cropping.
